How it works

1

Press your shortcut

Pick any key combo you like. The screen freezes and dims.

2

Drag around the text

Draw a box over a paragraph, a caption, or a whole page. Esc cancels.

3

Listen

It starts reading immediately and highlights each word as it goes.

Made to stay out of your way

Private by design

Reading and speaking happen on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, and screenshots are deleted when you quit.

Your speed, your voice

Slide from 0× to 4×, or type an exact speed. Choose any voice installed on your computer.

Click any word

Jump to any point in the text, or open your history to hear an earlier snip again.

Free and open source

No account, no subscription, no ads. The code is public under the MIT license.

Installing on Mac

First time setup
  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag SnipSpeech into Applications.
  2. Open SnipSpeech. If macOS says it can't verify the app, right-click the app, choose Open, then click Open. You only do this once.
  3. Choose your snip shortcut in the welcome window.
  4. The first time you snip, allow Screen Recording in System Settings › Privacy & Security. Then quit and reopen SnipSpeech.
Why does macOS show a warning?

SnipSpeech is a free, independent app and isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time. The right-click Open step above is the standard way to approve it. The full source code is on GitHub if you'd like to check it.

Does it work on Windows or Linux?

Not yet. They're planned. Star the project on GitHub to follow along.
